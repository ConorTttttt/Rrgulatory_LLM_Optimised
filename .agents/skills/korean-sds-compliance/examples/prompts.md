# Example Gemini 4 Hydrogen prompts

The following prompts are **tasks to run after activating** `korean-sds-compliance` and supplying the legal files and SDS evidence. They are not examples of a preapproved SDS.

## 1. Full SDS review

> Use the Korean SDS compliance skill to review this SDS against the source notice 2026-26 and all applicable annexes. Extract Sections 1–16, map each to Annex 4's field IDs, cite the original Korean requirement and SDS page for each defect, then provide a conservative findings table. Report missing evidence separately; no overall PASS without independent verification.

## 2. Section 3 mixture / ingredients

> Compare Section 3 ingredient declarations and any claimed confidential alternatives against Articles 11, 16–18 and Annexes 6–7. Distinguish confirmed ingredient concentrations, disclosure-omission cutoffs and GHS mixture classification. List every missing government approval document or unknown hazard group.

## 3. Korean warning label

> Review these actual warning-label photos alongside the SDS Section 2 and Annexes 2–3. Check stated container size and measured label/pictogram areas. Treat pictogram identity and any flattened HWP category table as requiring original visual verification. Do not certify the label based on SDS text alone.

## 4. Submission and use classification

> Determine whether Annex 5 use-code selection is mandatory for this specific SDS submission under Article 11(8). Validate the selected codes against the 79 entries. Separate preparing the SDS from legally submitting it; request official proof of submission where needed.

## 5. What additional documents do we need?

> Based on this SDS and its review scope, produce a ranked list of missing evidence that prevents an adequate legal or chemical conclusion, with exact source provisions and how each missing document would resolve a specific review question.

## 6. Bilingual regulatory summary

> Explain the cited Article 11(9) requirements in plain English but preserve the original Korean quotation. Distinguish the Annex 6 concentration limit for component-information omission from Annex 1 mixture classification thresholds. List any unresolved legal interpretation or exceptions.
